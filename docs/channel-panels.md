## How the stream works

> Every session has a goal and a short task list, shown on screen. I predict
> outcomes out loud, then we find out whether I was right.

## Chat roles

Give chat a part to play. Redeem and command ideas that fit the format:

- **Callers** are anyone in chat. They can call a prediction ("hit or miss?")
  before it's settled.
- **Testers** try the thing being built when it's ready.
- A channel point redeem to **pick the next task** from the list.
- A redeem to **name the prototype**.

## Rules

> Be kind, stay on topic, no spoilers for puzzles or games without asking,
> and no harassment of any kind.

Check the channel art, emotes, and titles against Twitch's Community Guidelines
before going live.
