# Channel panels

Copy-paste starting points for the Twitch channel page. Replace the bracketed
parts. Keep your own name visible somewhere so the serious work stays legible.

## About

> [Name], lead scientist at FartLabs. I build open source tools in public:
> [what you build]. The lab is live [days/times]. Pull up a stool.
>
> Code: https://github.com/FartLabs

## Schedule

> [Days] at [time + timezone]. 45 to 90 minutes, one goal per session, breaks
> every block. Schedule changes are announced in chat.

## How the lab works

> Every session has a goal and a short list of experiments, shown on screen.
> I predict outcomes out loud, then we find out whether I was right.

## Chat roles

Give chat a part to play. Redeem and command ideas that fit the format:

- **Lab assistants** are anyone in chat. They can call a prediction ("hit or
  miss?") before it's settled.
- **Test subjects** try the thing being built when it's ready.
- A channel point redeem to **pick the next experiment** from the list.
- A redeem to **name the prototype**.

## Rules

> Be kind, stay on topic, no spoilers for puzzles or games without asking,
> and no harassment of any kind.

Check the channel art, emotes, and titles against Twitch's Community Guidelines
before going live, since lab-coat humor is easy to push a little too far.
